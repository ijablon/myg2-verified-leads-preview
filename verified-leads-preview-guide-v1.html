Quick preview guide

How to explore this prototype

This is a clickable product demo for reviewing campaign health, lead opportunities, the Leads Exchange, purchased leads, and the Verified Leads MCP experience. The data is illustrative; no real purchases or lead delivery occur.

Why this prototype exists

The broader PPL concept is about helping vendors get more value from campaign budget that may otherwise go unused. The Overview makes budget pacing, targeting gaps, estimated qualified-lead volume, and the tradeoffs of a recommended change easier to review. The Leads Exchange adds another path to relevant demand when a buyer is not already part of the vendor’s normal campaign flow.

Discovery Leads represent earlier, less-qualified demand from buyer requests. Bronze, Silver, and Gold show progressively stronger signals about buyer intent or contact quality. Close Match Leads represent richer, advisor-qualified opportunities with more context for the vendor. The MCP area demonstrates how this experience could eventually be accessed through tools such as ChatGPT or Claude, not only through the dashboard.

The showcase discussed these as product concepts and monetization paths; pricing, batch rules, and final operational details remain illustrative in this prototype.

Start with the main navigation

  • Overview: review campaign status, growth opportunities, and close-match lead previews.
  • Campaign Health: inspect a recommended targeting change and enable or undo it.
  • The Leads Exchange: compare available Close Match and Discovery opportunities.
  • Leads: review purchased and delivered leads with full buyer information.
  • Verified Leads MCP: try the sample assistant prompts and result cards.

Useful interactions

  • Use Preview, View Lead, or View Details to open lead information.
  • Available leads keep buyer contact information anonymized until purchase.
  • Purchased leads show full buyer details and advisor context.
  • Use Reset demo in the top-right to return the prototype to its starting state.

Lead types at a glance

Bronze Discovery

Basic request signal: the buyer submitted a software-selection request.

Silver Discovery

Competitor research signal: the buyer viewed a specific competitor.

Gold Discovery

Verified contact signal: an advisor confirmed the contact information is accurate.

Close Match leads include richer advisor-qualified context and are presented with anonymized buyer information before purchase.

Suggested walkthrough

  1. Open Overview and review the campaign health recommendation.
  2. Preview a Close Match lead and review the advisor notes.
  3. Open The Leads Exchange and compare Bronze, Silver, and Gold Discovery cards.
  4. Open Leads and inspect a purchased Verified, Close Match, and Discovery lead.
  5. Try one of the sample prompts in Verified Leads MCP.
