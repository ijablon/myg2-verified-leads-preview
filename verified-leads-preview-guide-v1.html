Quick preview guide

How to explore this prototype

This clickable product demo presents a new MyG2 dashboard experience for the Verified Leads program. It is designed for performance marketers, demand generation managers, and paid media managers, not sales teams. The data is illustrative; no real purchases or lead delivery occur.

Why this prototype exists

The prototype is designed to address two related problems that Verified Leads faces.

Budget utilization

Vendors may have campaign budget that remains unused when there are not enough Verified Leads available to sell.

Overly narrow campaign setup

Sellers can over-filter their campaigns, which limits the number of qualified buyer opportunities they can reach.

What the prototype demonstrates

Dashboard health status

The monthly health bar shows whether the seller is on pace based on Verified Leads received that month.

One-click Discovery Lead activation

Enable Discovery Leads for the campaign and automatically receive eligible leads that fit the campaign ICP, including recent leads and new leads going forward.

Lead Growth Opportunities

Select multiple recommendations, make the targeting changes in one click, and see the eligible lead count and related budget callouts update. Eligible recent leads missed because targeting was too narrow are offered at a discounted rate.

AI Scanner

Click Create Campaign to let the website-based scanner do the campaign setup work and produce a campaign ready to activate, with the goal of three-click campaign activation.

Lead types at a glance

Verified Leads

Advisor-qualified leads presented as part of the seller's Verified Leads program.

Close Match Leads

Richer opportunities that closely match the seller's target and include additional qualification context. Contact information is anonymized until purchase in a preview.

Discovery Leads

Earlier buyer requests available in three tiers. Bronze, Silver, and Gold belong only to Discovery Leads.

Bronze: the basic Discovery signalSilver: the buyer viewed a specific competitorGold: an advisor confirmed the contact information is accurate

The Discovery tiers provide different levels of buyer-intent or contact-quality information and are priced differently.

Leads Exchange: two separate experiences

Verified Leads / ProDashboard widget

The prototype shows a Leads Exchange widget on the dashboard. Sellers can scroll to the widget, preview a Close Match Lead, and click Buy this lead for a one-off purchase.

StarterSeparate Leads Exchange

Starter sellers are not part of the Verified Leads dashboard. They can purchase Close Match Leads or Discovery Leads, filter by ICP and Discovery tier, and preview matching leads.

Verified Leads MCP

The core users are performance marketers, demand generation managers, and paid media managers. They may not log into MyG2 frequently, so the Verified Leads MCP would let them interact with campaigns from tools such as Claude or ChatGPT.

The MCP could help them analyze campaign performance, adjust targeting, set up campaigns, and search for or purchase Close Match and Discovery Leads without leaving the environment where they already work. This builds on G2's existing experience with MCPs for buyer-intent data.

Suggested prototype walkthrough

  1. Start at the dashboard health status and review whether the seller is on pace.
  2. Enable Discovery Leads and note that the seller is opting into eligible recent and future Discovery Leads based on the ICP.
  3. Open Lead Growth Opportunities. Select one or more recommendations and observe the lead count and budget callouts update as the changes are combined. Review eligible recent leads offered at a discount.
  4. From the top of the dashboard, click Create Campaign to open the AI Scanner. Review how it does the campaign setup work and leads toward three-click campaign activation.
  5. If reviewing the Pro experience, scroll to the Leads Exchange widget, preview a Close Match Lead, and click Buy this lead.
  6. If reviewing the Starter experience, open the separate Leads Exchange, filter Close Match or Discovery Leads by ICP and Discovery tier, and preview a matching lead.
  7. Open the Verified Leads MCP experience and review how campaign and lead actions could happen through chat.
